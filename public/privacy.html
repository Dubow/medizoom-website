Website privacy notice

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Your privacy matters to MediZoom. This notice explains what personal information is involved when you visit medizoom.care or contact us, why it is used and how to raise a privacy question.

1. Introduction and scope

MediZoom is developing a telemedicine platform for Kenya. This notice applies to the current pre-launch information website and email enquiries, including requests for private demonstrations from healthcare professionals and potential partners.

It does not cover patient accounts, clinical records, consultations or payments in the planned mobile service. Those services are not available through this website and will require separate privacy information before public use. Please also read our Terms and Services and medical disclaimer.

2. Information we receive

  • Website visits. Our website hosting and delivery providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser information, requested pages and access times to deliver and protect the website.
  • Email correspondence. If you contact us, we receive your email address, any name or professional details you choose to provide, your message and any attachments you send. This includes general enquiries and private-demo requests.

There are no account registration, contact or payment forms on this website. Emailing us is optional. You can read the website without sending an enquiry; without a reply address or enough information about a request, we may be unable to respond.

3. How information is used

  • To respond to general, professional and privacy enquiries.
  • To review private-demo requests and arrange demonstrations manually, subject to availability.
  • To manage the correspondence that follows an enquiry.
  • To deliver the website, investigate technical problems and protect against misuse.
  • To meet applicable legal requirements concerning information that must be retained or disclosed.

We do not sell enquiry information or treat an enquiry as permission to send marketing messages. Requesting a demonstration does not create an account or grant automatic application access.

4. Basis for processing

Website operation, security and handling enquiries rely on the legitimate interests of providing an information website and responding to people who contact us, subject to their rights. Where a legal obligation requires information to be retained or disclosed, that obligation may also apply.

5. Service providers and sharing

The following categories of service providers are involved in handling website and enquiry information:

  • Website hosting and delivery providers: process technical request information to make the website available and support its operation and security.
  • Email delivery and storage providers: process contact details, messages and attachments so that enquiries can be received, stored and answered.

These providers may use service partners in delivering their services. Information may also need to be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

You can contact us for further information about the providers relevant to your information and how it is handled.

6. Retention

The appropriate retention period depends on the purpose of the information, whether correspondence or an issue remains unresolved, and any applicable legal requirement. Hosting logs, email storage and backups also depend on service-provider retention settings and terms.

You can ask about the retention of your information or request deletion using the privacy contact below. A request may be subject to applicable legal requirements to retain information.

7. Processing outside Kenya

Our service providers and their service partners may process information outside Kenya. This notice does not promise Kenya-only storage or processing.

Cross-border processing is subject to applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements. You can request information about the relevant recipients, processing locations and safeguards through our privacy contact.

8. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts. It does not set application account cookies or use browser storage to track visitor behaviour.

Clicking an email link opens your email application; it does not submit a website form or subscribe you to a mailing list. Your email application and external websites have their own privacy practices.

9. Security and sensitive information

This website uses HTTPS to protect information in transit between your browser and the website. No website or email service can guarantee absolute security.

Please do not send medical records, prescriptions, identity documents, professional licences or information about another person's health to the general enquiry address. It is not a clinical intake channel or an emergency service.

10. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable Kenyan law, you can ask to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is used.
  • Access personal information held about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or misleading information.
  • Object to processing or request deletion where applicable.

Send a privacy request to support@medizoom.care. Describe your request without attaching identity or health documents. We may need proportionate information to confirm that the request relates to you.

You can also raise a concern with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. Its guidance explains data subject rights.

11. Children's information

This website provides general information. It does not offer accounts for children or request their personal or health information. A parent or guardian concerned about information sent to us can contact us using the privacy address below.

12. External websites

Links to professional registers and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy notices. This notice does not describe or control those services. Review their notices before sharing information with them.

13. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when the website's data practices change and show the revision date above. Any future app registration or clinical service will need its own privacy information before use.

14. Contact MediZoom

For questions about this notice or the handling of website enquiry information, contact the MediZoom team at support@medizoom.care.
