Medical disclaimer

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Not for emergencies. If you need urgent medical attention, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest appropriate emergency facility. Do not wait for a reply from MediZoom.

1. Purpose and scope

MediZoom is developing a telemedicine platform for Kenya. This disclaimer applies to the pre-launch information website at medizoom.care, general enquiries and private product demonstrations. Public consultations and bookings are not open.

Descriptions of the planned patient journey and doctor experience explain the platform's intended purpose. They are not an offer of immediate medical care or a substitute for the terms and consent required for a future clinical service.

2. Information, not medical advice

This website does not provide personalised medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or prescriptions. Its content is general information and does not take account of your medical history, symptoms or individual circumstances.

Do not start, stop or change treatment based solely on this website. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional about your health, and do not disregard professional advice or delay seeking care because of something you read here.

3. No clinical relationship

Browsing the website, sending an enquiry or attending a private product demonstration does not establish a patient-doctor relationship. Private demonstrations explain the product; they are not medical consultations, assessments or treatment sessions.

Demonstration requests are reviewed and arranged manually. A request does not confirm a clinical appointment or grant automatic application access.

4. Limits of remote care

The planned online service will not be suitable for every health concern and is not intended for emergencies. A clinician may need an in-person examination, tests or a referral before deciding on appropriate care.

A description of remote consultations on this website does not mean that a particular condition can be assessed or treated online. The suitability of remote care requires an individual clinical assessment.

5. Prescriptions and outcomes

Describing prescription or follow-up features does not guarantee a prescription, diagnosis, referral or particular health outcome. Any future prescribing or treatment decision must depend on an appropriate clinical assessment, professional judgement and applicable requirements.

The website and product demonstrations cannot issue a valid prescription or authorise you to obtain or use a medicine.

6. Doctor information and credential checks

Our website describes a planned credential-review process. It does not claim that any doctor has already been verified through that process, or that MediZoom has received regulatory approval or endorsement. Professional registration and a current practising licence are distinct matters.

For independent practitioner information, consult the official Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC) registers. Linking to the registers does not imply an affiliation with or endorsement by the Council.

7. Illustrations and product information

The homepage consultation imagery is illustrative, not a photograph of a participating MediZoom doctor or a real patient consultation. Product descriptions are not clinical evidence, patient testimonials or a promise of treatment results.

Features and availability may change during development. Information about a planned service should not be used to postpone care that is already needed.

8. Contact and sensitive information

For non-clinical questions or a correction to this website, email support@medizoom.care. The general support email is not monitored as an emergency or clinical service and should not be used to request diagnosis, treatment or urgent assistance.

Please do not send symptoms for assessment, prescriptions, patient records, identity documents or another person's medical information through this contact channel. The privacy notice explains how website visits and email enquiries involve personal information.

9. Your rights

This disclaimer explains the limits of the current website. It does not waive professional duties or exclude or limit rights, remedies or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law.

10. Updates and related information

We will publish changes to this disclaimer here with an updated revision date. Please also read our Terms and Services.

Separate service terms, privacy information and any required clinical consent will be provided before public clinical services become available. This disclaimer is not consent to treatment or a replacement for that information.
