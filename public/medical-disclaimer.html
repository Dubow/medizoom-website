Medical disclaimer

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Not for emergencies. If you need urgent medical attention, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest appropriate emergency facility. Do not wait for a reply from MediZoom.

Information, not medical advice

This website introduces a telemedicine platform in development. Its content is not personalised medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or a prescription. Public consultations and bookings are not open.

Do not use this website to make decisions about starting, stopping or changing treatment. Speak to a qualified healthcare professional about your individual circumstances, and do not delay seeking care because of something you read here.

No clinical relationship through this website

Browsing this website or sending an enquiry does not establish a patient-doctor relationship. The general support email is not monitored as an emergency or clinical service. Please do not send symptoms for assessment, prescriptions, patient records or urgent medical requests.

Limits of the planned online service

An online consultation will not be suitable for every concern. A clinician may need an in-person examination, tests or referral before deciding on appropriate care. Describing prescriptions or follow-up features does not guarantee a prescription, diagnosis or particular outcome.

Doctor information and credential checks

Our website describes a planned credential-review process. It does not claim that any doctor has already been verified through that process, or that MediZoom has received regulatory approval or endorsement. Professional registration and a current practising licence are distinct matters.

For independent practitioner information, consult the official KMPDC registers. The homepage consultation imagery is illustrative, not a photograph of a participating MediZoom doctor or a real consultation.
