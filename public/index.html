A little more
about Medizoom.

Is Medizoom available yet?

Medizoom launches in Kenya on 20 December 2026. Appointments and consultations are not yet open.

Who is Medizoom for?

Medizoom is being designed for patients looking for a more connected way to manage care, and doctors looking to coordinate their availability, consultations and patient communication.

How can I enquire as a doctor?

Contact support@medizoom.care for professional enquiries. Please don't send licences, identity documents or patient information by email. This contact channel is not a registration or verification service.

What information does this website collect?

This website has no registration form, analytics scripts or advertising trackers. Email links open your email application, and information you send is handled through email. Once hosted, the hosting provider may process technical request logs.
