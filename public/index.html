The planned patient journey

How MediZoom works.

From choosing a doctor to returning to your records, here's the experience we're preparing for launch.

  1. 01

    Find your doctor.

    Explore professional profiles and specialties to find a doctor for your consultation.

  2. 02

    Arrange your visit.

    Choose an available time, review the consultation fee and complete the booking and payment steps in the app.

  3. 03

    Meet online.

    Join your scheduled video consultation. Your doctor can assess whether an in-person visit is needed.

  4. 04

    Keep care connected.

    Return to your visit records, download any prescription your doctor issues and share relevant documents in your conversation.

Online consultations are not suitable for every health concern and do not guarantee a prescription. MediZoom is not an emergency service. Read our medical disclaimer.

Preparing for responsible care

Professional credentials.
Careful review.

Our planned doctor onboarding process will distinguish professional registration from a current annual practising licence, with review before doctors become available to patients.

Submit credentials.

Doctors will be asked for their registration certificate, current practising licence and supporting specialist evidence where applicable.

Check and review.

We plan to check the submitted details against the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC) register and request clarification where needed.

Keep details current.

Annual licence renewals will require updated evidence and review. Availability on MediZoom is intended to depend on current, accepted credentials.

This describes a planned process, not a claim that doctor checks or regulatory approvals have been completed. A listing will not replace your own checks or guarantee a clinical outcome.

Your questions, answered

A little more
about MediZoom.

Is MediZoom available yet?

Targeting February 2027 for launch in Kenya. This is a target, not a confirmed opening date. Appointments and consultations are not yet open. We will confirm availability here once launch preparations are complete.

Who is MediZoom for?

MediZoom is being designed for patients looking for a more connected way to manage care, and doctors looking to coordinate their availability, consultations and patient communication.

How can I enquire as a doctor?

Contact support@medizoom.care for professional enquiries. Please don't send licences, identity documents or patient information by email. This contact channel is not a registration or verification service.

Can I request a private demonstration?

Healthcare professionals and potential partners can request a private demo through our existing support email. We review requests and arrange demonstrations manually, subject to availability. This does not open an account or grant automatic application access.

How will doctors be reviewed?

We plan to review registration, current practising licences and applicable specialist evidence before a doctor becomes available to patients, with ongoing licence updates. Read about the planned review process. This is not a statement of KMPDC endorsement.

Can I get medical help through this website?

No. This is an information website, not a consultation or emergency service. Please seek urgent medical care locally if you need immediate help, and do not wait for an email response. Medical disclaimer.

What information does this website collect?

There are no registration forms, analytics scripts or advertising trackers in this website. Our hosting provider may process technical request logs. Emailing us shares the information you include in your message. Read the website privacy notice.
