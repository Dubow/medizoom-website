About this website

MediZoom is developing a telemedicine platform for Kenya. This website introduces the planned experience and provides a general enquiry address. It does not accept appointments or payments and does not provide medical advice or consultations.

The planned launch is 20 December 2026. The date, features and availability may change as preparations progress. A description on this website is not an offer of an immediately available clinical service or a guarantee of access to a particular doctor.

Appropriate use

General enquiries do not create a patient-doctor relationship, a confirmed appointment or a doctor participation agreement. Do not send clinical records, identity documents or professional credentials through the general contact email.
