# MediZoom

**Care, closer to you.**

MediZoom is developing a telemedicine platform for Kenya, bringing doctor
discovery, consultations and care records into a connected experience for
patients and healthcare professionals.

## Our Focus

- **Finding care:** helping patients explore doctor profiles and arrange consultations.
- **Continuity of care:** keeping conversations, shared documents and visit information together.
- **Supporting clinical practice:** giving doctors a dedicated space to coordinate availability, appointments and follow-up.

These describe the platform in development, not services currently available
through the website.

## Preparing for Launch

**Targeting February 2027 for launch in Kenya.** Timing is subject to service
readiness. Public appointments and consultations are not yet open; availability
will be announced at [medizoom.care](https://medizoom.care/).

## Healthcare Professionals and Partners

Healthcare professionals and prospective partners can
[request a private demonstration](mailto:support@medizoom.care?subject=MediZoom%20private%20demo%20request)
to learn more about the planned experience. Requests are reviewed and
demonstrations arranged individually, subject to availability.
